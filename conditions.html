Clinical Overview
Inflammation or infection of the middle ear space behind the eardrum, often triggered by upper respiratory tract infections or Eustachian tube dysfunction. Frequent in both pediatric and adult populations.
Common Symptoms
Ear pain, hearing loss, fluid drainage, fever.
Treatment Pathway
Antibiotics, ear drops, or myringotomy tubes if persistent.

Clinical Overview
The perception of sound when no external acoustic stimulus is present. May stem from hair cell damage in the cochlea, noise exposure, vascular anomalies, or acoustic neuroma.
Common Symptoms
Ringing, buzzing, or hissing sounds in the ear.
Treatment Pathway
Sound therapy, counseling, and underlying cause management.
Schedule Tinnitus Assessment
Clinical Overview
Displacement of the nasal septum (the bone and cartilage dividing the nasal cavity), obstructing airflow and increasing susceptibility to recurrent sinus infections.
Common Symptoms
Nasal congestion, nosebleeds, headaches, snoring.
Treatment Pathway
Nasal sprays or Septoplasty surgery.
Schedule Septal Evaluation
Clinical Overview
Soft, painless, noncancerous growths on the lining of nasal passages or sinuses associated with chronic inflammation, asthma, recurring infections, or aspirin sensitivity.
Common Symptoms
Runny nose, persistent stuffiness, decreased sense of smell.
Treatment Pathway
Steroid sprays, biologics, or endoscopic sinus surgery.
Schedule Nasal Endoscopy
Clinical Overview
Often known as "silent reflux," LPR occurs when gastric acid flows up through the esophagus into the larynx and pharynx, irritating delicate vocal cords without typical heartburn sensation.
Common Symptoms
Chronic throat clearing, lump sensation, hoarseness.
Treatment Pathway
Dietary changes and proton pump inhibitors.
